TPMidi

MIDI control surface with four rotary encoders, three 75mm faders, and an OLED display. Assign Control Change, Program Change, and DAW transport controls (play, stop, undo) to any control. Live feedback on the OLED shows current parameter values and assignments. Fully compatible with any DAW that supports MIDI.

Encoders 4 rotary with display
Faders 3 × 75mm assignable
Mapping CC, Program Change, Transport
