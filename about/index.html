About TProduction

We design and build MIDI control hardware for music producers. Every product is designed with one goal: get out of your way. No bloat, no unnecessary features—just precise, responsive controls that work with any DAW.

Our hardware is built around the core belief that the best tool is one that disappears into the creative process. When you're focused on your music, your gear should respond instantly and reliably. That's why we use studio-grade components, test extensively, and maintain minimal but complete feature sets.

Our Values

Precision

Every control responds exactly as expected, with minimal latency and zero drift. Professional-grade components throughout.

Simplicity

No screens, confusing menus, or mandatory software. Plug in, map, and create. Class-compliant MIDI works everywhere.

Reliability

Built to last. Designed for daily studio use and small live rigs. No compromises on component quality.

Compatibility

Works with every major DAW on macOS, Windows, and Linux. No special drivers needed. USB MIDI is the standard.

Why Hardware?

Software control is fine, but there's something irreplaceable about tactile feedback. A fader you can grab. A button you can feel. The muscle memory that builds over months of use. Hardware doesn't replace your software—it augments it, giving you faster access to the parameters you change most.

TPMidi and TPFader are designed to sit next to your keyboard, ready when you need them. They're compact enough for laptop studios, but precise enough for professional mixing and sound design work.
