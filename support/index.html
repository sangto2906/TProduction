Drivers & Compatibility

Both TPMidi and TPFader use class-compliant USB MIDI. No drivers needed on macOS, Windows 7+, or Linux.

macOS: Works out of the box on 10.5 and later. Audio MIDI Setup recognized immediately.

Windows: Plug in and use. Windows 7 and later have native MIDI support.

Linux: ALSA and PulseAudio fully supported. Test with aconnect -o to verify connection.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I remap controls?

Use your DAW's native MIDI learning. Open the DAW's controller settings, click "Learn", and move a TPFader or TPMidi control. The control will automatically map to that parameter. No extra software required.

Can I use TPFader and TPMidi together?

Yes. Both units connect via separate USB ports and appear as distinct MIDI controllers to your DAW. You can map them to different channels and use them simultaneously.

Is there a firmware update process?

Updates are simple. Download the firmware file, connect the unit via USB, and follow the update tool instructions. No special technical knowledge required. Updates are optional; the original firmware is stable.

What DAWs are supported?

All DAWs that support standard MIDI control: Ableton Live, Logic Pro, Pro Tools, Reaper, Studio One, Bitwig, FL Studio, Cubase, and more. We provide mapping templates for the most popular ones.

How do I troubleshoot connection issues?

First, try a different USB cable and port. Then check Audio MIDI Setup (macOS) or Device Manager (Windows) to see if the unit appears. If problems persist, contact support with your OS and DAW version.

Are TPFader and TPMidi hackable / modifiable?

Both units are designed to be reliable rather than modular. We provide stable firmware and full MIDI documentation. For advanced needs, reach out to us—we may have other solutions.
